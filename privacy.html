Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Download Youtube Thumbnail ("we," "this site") is a free tool for downloading YouTube video thumbnails. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data when you use it.

Data we collect

None. Download Youtube Thumbnail has no backend server, no database, and no user accounts. There is no form on this site that sends data to us for storage.

How the thumbnail downloader works

When you paste a YouTube URL, it's read entirely by JavaScript running in your own browser to extract the video ID — that URL is never sent to us or logged anywhere. Your browser then requests the thumbnail images directly from YouTube's servers (img.youtube.com); we never see, receive, or store the videos or links you look up.

Third-party services

Because thumbnail images are fetched directly from Google's servers, that request is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may log standard web server access data (such as IP address) as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (such as which pages are viewed and how often). Google Analytics collects information like your approximate location, device and browser type, and pages visited — it does not collect the YouTube URLs you look up. You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy and opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets cookies to distinguish visitors and sessions. We don't use cookies for advertising, and no other part of this site sets cookies.

Children's privacy

Download Youtube Thumbnail is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
