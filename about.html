About Us

A free tool for grabbing YouTube video thumbnails in every resolution YouTube provides — no account, no software, no watermark.

YouTube generates several sizes of a thumbnail for every video it hosts, from a small 120×90 preview up to a full 1280×720 HD image, but there's no built-in way to download them. Download Youtube Thumbnail fills that gap: paste a video link and get every size instantly.

How it works

  1. Paste a YouTube video link (a normal youtube.com/watch link, a youtu.be short link, or a Shorts link all work).
  2. Download Youtube Thumbnail reads the video ID out of the link in your browser and builds the thumbnail image URLs — nothing is uploaded to a server.
  3. Preview every available resolution and click Download to save the one you want.

Why use this tool

  • Free, with no limits or sign-up
  • Nothing is uploaded — the video ID is extracted locally, and thumbnails are fetched directly from YouTube's own servers
  • No watermark added to any image
  • Works for any public YouTube video, including Shorts
